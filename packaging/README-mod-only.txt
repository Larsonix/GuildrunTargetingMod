GUILDRUN TARGETING PREVIEW  v{VERSION}  (mod only)
A mod for the Guildrun Demo, made by Larsonix.


READ THIS FIRST

This zip contains only the mod, without MelonLoader. Take it if you
already have MelonLoader installed, for example if you already used
this mod before, or if you use other mods.

If you never installed MelonLoader, take the other zip instead :
GuildrunTargetingMod-v{VERSION}-with-MelonLoader.zip


WHAT IT DOES

See how the fight is going to start, while you are still placing your
heroes.

Hover a unit to see where it ends up standing and who it ends up
fighting. Hold a hero and the whole board answers for the hex under
your cursor, so you see the result before you drop it. A toggle shows
the whole board at once. Items, relics and abilities that only pay
from a certain place are marked when they are not paying.

The mod does not change the fight. It only shows what was already
going to happen.


SCORES AND LEADERBOARDS

  A run played with the mod on cannot submit a score and does not
  move your Red Rift win streak in either direction.

While the mod is switched on, your scores do not go to the Steam
leaderboards. Not the Endless score, and not the Red Rift win streak.
On a Red Rift run played with the mod on, a win does not advance the
streak and a loss or abandon does not reset it.

You still see the leaderboards. Your standing is never lowered, and
your runs, your unlocks and your progress are saved the way they
always were.


INSTALL

1. In Steam : right click Guildrun Demo > Manage > Browse local files.
2. Drag the Mods folder from this zip into that folder.
   If Windows asks to replace files, say yes.
3. Launch the game normally.

The preview works from your very first placement.


ALREADY HAVE AN OLDER VERSION

Nothing to uninstall. Updating is the same three steps above : say yes
when Windows asks to replace the file. If the game is open, restart it,
because the new file is only read when the game starts.

The mod is a single file replaced in place, so nothing from the old
version is left behind. Your settings, your shortcuts and your progress
are kept : this zip carries no settings file, and it does not touch
MelonLoader.


TURNING IT ON AND OFF

There is a button in the main menu that says TARGETING MOD: ON or
TARGETING MOD: OFF. Press it and confirm. You never have to close the
game and you never have to delete anything.

Switched off, the mod does nothing at all while you play, and your
scores go to the leaderboards again under the rule above.


REMOVE IT

Delete Mods\GuildrunTargetingMod.dll in the game folder. Your other
mods and MelonLoader stay untouched.

To play for the leaderboards, switching the mod off in the main menu
is enough.
